# Watch Your Health (WYH) - Business Analyst Case Study

## Overview

This repository presents a comprehensive Business Analyst case study conducted on Watch Your Health (WYH), a leading HealthTech and InsurTech platform.

The objective of this project was to analyze the company's business model, evaluate market positioning, identify strategic opportunities, design executive dashboards, define KPIs, and create business analysis artifacts that support decision-making and business growth.

The project demonstrates practical Business Analysis skills including requirement gathering, stakeholder thinking, KPI design, dashboard planning, competitor analysis, process improvement, and strategic recommendations.

---

## Business Problem

HealthTech organizations face several challenges:

- Low user engagement
- Limited visibility into business performance
- High claims costs
- Customer retention challenges
- Manual reporting processes
- Fragmented healthcare data
- Difficulty identifying high-risk users

This case study explores how data-driven decision making and business analysis practices can address these challenges.
